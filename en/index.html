What is pelvic physiotherapy?

It is the physiotherapy specialty focused on the prevention and treatment of intimate dysfunctions, mainly through the function of the pelvic floor muscles. Just like any other muscle group in our body, this region can also be tense or weakened and need special care.

When should you see a pelvic physiotherapist?

Every woman, at any stage of life, should have at least one appointment to get to know her body better and identify possible risk factors, helping to prevent intimate dysfunctions. Or if you experience:

Urine or stool leakage – prevention and treatment regardless of age or circumstances

Urgency to go to the toilet – having to rush to the bathroom in fear is not normal

Difficulty emptying your bowels – constipation, even chronic, can improve with a few physiotherapy adjustments.

Pain during intercourse – feeling pain or burning, or even avoiding sex, no longer has to happen

Reduced pleasure and low lubrication – there are many ways to improve your intimate well-being and satisfaction

Discomfort caused by endometriosis – less pain and a better quality of life

Physiotherapy in Obstetrics

There are two more special moments when a woman benefits greatly from the care of a pelvic physiotherapist: pregnancy and the postpartum period!

Beyond the function of the intimate region, during this period our attention turns to the mother's comfort, birth preparation and baby care, whatever the mode of birth. My care is individualised so that each woman receives the approach best suited to her body and moment: relieving the common discomforts of pregnancy (lower back, hip and pubic pain, swollen legs), preparing the perineum for birth to prevent tears, caring for the abdomen to treat diastasis after birth, adapting exercise to avoid injury and helping to prevent gestational diabetes and pre-eclampsia — as well as sharing scientific knowledge in a simple way so that each woman has the autonomy to make her own decisions at such an important time in her family's life.
